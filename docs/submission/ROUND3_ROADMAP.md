# Relay: roadmap

| Horizon | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Next** | Nightly backups with a scheduled restore drill; Memory Replay for repositories and assets; sentence-level brief diffs | Operational confidence; history beyond a single responsibility |
| **Soon** | Calendar and HR signals feeding the simulator; Slack threads and Jira issues as evidence; pgvector retrieval for Ask Relay; Linear and Notion webhooks with the GitHub-style sync checkpoint; saved multi-person What If? scenarios; scheduled continuity reviews per team | Absences known in advance; more evidence; faster freshness; planning reorganisations |
| **Later** | Organisation-wide resilience trends; SCIM provisioning; self-hosted models for regulated customers | Enterprise scale and compliance |

Each item and the trigger for building it: [scope and next steps](ROUND3_SCOPE_AND_NEXT_STEPS.md).

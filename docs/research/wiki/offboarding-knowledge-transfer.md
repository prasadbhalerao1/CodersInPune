# Offboarding and knowledge transfer

## Key Properties
- **Vendor guidance (Asana, Kayako, HR Cloud):** checklists cover access, assets, pay and one knowledge-transfer item; IT should prepare access removal but not execute it until the handover is complete.
- **Vendor claims:** Sensay markets an AI offboarding platform built on adaptive interviews; Enterprise Knowledge built an asynchronous interview agent for departing experts.
- **Research claims (secondary citations, not verified at source):** APQC reports only 8% of organisations consistently capture departing employees' knowledge; eGain cites Deloitte at 92% failing to.
- **Consensus across sources:** AI helps structure capture; tacit knowledge still needs people.

## Where they stop
Transfer is a checklist item. Nothing checks that the knowledge reached a named successor or that ownership actually moved.

## Relevance to Relay
Relay's handover interview, per-recipient brief, acknowledgement and verified execution fill that gap. Opportunity: start capture when key-person exposure appears, not at resignation.

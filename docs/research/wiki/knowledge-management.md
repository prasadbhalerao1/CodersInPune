# Knowledge management and verification

## Key Properties
- **Verified (Guru docs):** each card has a verifier (person or group); re-verification intervals run from weekly to yearly or never; unverified cards stay searchable but are flagged; unverified and unused cards go to an auto-archive queue; AI can suggest a verifier.
- **Assumption:** Notion, Slab and Trainual offer page ownership, verification or training paths; not re-verified in this pass.

## Where they stop
They know what is written down, not who is accountable for keeping work running, nor what breaks when that person leaves.

## Relevance to Relay
Relay already has review cadences and stale-knowledge risks. Rejected: auto-archiving (Relay should never silently remove context). Adopted: freshness is tied to the responsibility's owner, not to a document's author.

# Relay's competitive position and what to build next

## Answer
Catalogues ([[service-catalogues]]) own software ownership by team; knowledge tools ([[knowledge-management]], [[ai-knowledge-systems]]) own what is written; offboarding tools ([[offboarding-knowledge-transfer]]) own the checklist; incident tools ([[incident-and-oncall]]) own the rota. None checks that accountability actually moved to a named successor and took hold. [[relay]] already covers ownership, risk, history, simulation and audited transfer; the council (five advisors, five anonymous reviewers, 2026-11-12) agreed unanimously that its weakest link was the missing post-transfer proof.

## Decisions
- **BUILD (done):** follow-through per transferred responsibility (holder recorded, reviewed or recorded activity since, tasks closed), measuring the work and never an individual.
- **BUILD (done):** key-person exposure, restricted to managers and auditors.
- **KEEP:** deterministic simulation, cited AI with "Not in the evidence", permission filtering before retrieval.
- **FIX (next):** a dated trigger: planned leave dates feeding the simulator and risks.
- **REJECT for now:** HRIS suite integrations, compliance packaging, Jira connector, Slack bot.
